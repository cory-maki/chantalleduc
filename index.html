Chantal Leduc

Fashion Stylist & Designer

Chantal Leduc is a French-Canadian fashion stylist, designer, and former international model from Montreal. From the front rows of Milan and Paris Fashion Week to the grand hotels and historic streets of Europe, she brings a refined, deeply personal eye to style, design, and the art of living well.

About Chantal

A life shaped by beauty and elegance

Born and raised in Montreal, Chantal Leduc stepped into fashion at a young age and built an international modeling career that carried her across continents. Those years introduced her to some of the industry’s most inspiring creative minds and shaped the eye that now defines her work as a fashion stylist and designer.

Today, Chantal’s aesthetic balances classic sophistication with contemporary luxury. A regular presence at Women’s Fashion Week in Milan and Paris, she follows celebrated houses and emerging designers alike, gathering lessons from the couture front row and translating them into style that feels effortless and personal.

Beyond the runway, Leduc is drawn to travel, architecture, and beautifully designed spaces. Above all, she is the proud mother of four accomplished adult children, her greatest source of joy.
