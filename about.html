Style & Design

Fashion styling with a refined, personal eye

Chantal Leduc is a French-Canadian fashion stylist and designer whose perspective has been refined through decades in the fashion industry. Today her focus is styling and design, where she combines a lifetime of international experience with an innate sense of refinement and individuality. She approaches every look the way a designer approaches a collection: with a clear point of view, an attention to proportion, and a respect for craftsmanship.

Her aesthetic draws from both classic sophistication and contemporary luxury. The result is a style that feels effortlessly elegant yet deeply personal, never a costume and always an expression of the woman wearing it. It is a point of view she explores in her philosophy of effortless elegance, where restraint, quality, and individuality matter far more than any single trend.

Beginnings

From Montreal to runways around the world

Born and raised in Montreal, Canada, Chantal began her journey in fashion at a young age. An international modeling career soon took her across continents and introduced her to some of the industry’s most inspiring creative influences, from photographers and stylists to the designers whose work she wore.

Along the way, she won Star Search International as a Spokesmodel in 1993, an early recognition of the poise and presence that still define her. Those formative years cultivated an enduring love for fashion, taught her how clothes live and move on the body, and laid the foundation for the work she pursues today.

Fashion Week

A regular presence in Milan and Paris

Each season, Leduc returns to Women’s Fashion Week in Milan and Paris, including the couture presentations in Paris. There she draws inspiration from the world’s most celebrated fashion houses, from emerging designers, and from the evolving artistic movements that shape what comes next. Her travels keep her connected to the pulse of global fashion while continually enriching her creative vision.

She shares moments from the shows with her followers on Instagram, offering a glimpse of the craftsmanship and spectacle behind each collection. Her journal gathers the front-row lessons from Milan and Paris that she carries home each season and translates into her own styling work.

Travel & Architecture

Inspired by beautifully designed places

Beyond the world of couture, Chantal’s passions extend to travel and architecture. She is captivated by beautifully designed spaces, historic residences, grand European hotels, and the architectural details that tell the story of a place. To her, a well-proportioned room and a well-cut gown speak the same language.

Whether strolling through the streets of Paris, discovering hidden gems in Milan, or exploring destinations further afield, she finds inspiration where design, culture, and history meet. She reflects on how travel sharpens a designer’s eye and why the places she visits shape the way she dresses.

Family & Living Well

Her greatest pride

Above all, Chantal’s greatest pride is her family. The mother of four accomplished adult children, she considers them her most meaningful achievement and her greatest source of joy.

Their accomplishments, character, and close bond remain at the center of her life. Today, Chantal embodies a lifestyle defined by elegance, creativity, and curiosity. Through her work and travels, she continues to celebrate the beauty of exceptional design, meaningful experiences, and the enduring influence of style in all its forms.
